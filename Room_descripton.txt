Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
vacuuming. There is an interesting tool in here as well. 
Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your
bedroom, and east to the kitchen.
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
occupies the far corner. The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of
clothes. There is also this weird looking key in my room as well.
 You can return south to the living room.
#
Kitchen 
This is where all the meals, feasts, and other food 
like resources are held. There is a flashlight, a knife laying out and
a mysterious orb just here for an odd ball reason(no pun intended). 
There "Garage" south from here or return back west to the LivingRoom.
#
Office
This is your fathers, using the office key that was left in my Bedroom, I was
able to get in. Office where he conducts his work, file management, and research
is done in this space. In this office there is a fairly old computer, and a strange
safe locked away as well. The LivingRoom is east from here if you'd like to return back. 
#
Garage
This room is housing and keeping place for the car. You could return north to the Kitchen,
if you'd like. 
#
Attic
This room at the top of the house being the epitome of storage unit. All the family items
and objects are stored here. Could use a cleaning though. If you'd like, you could return 
south to your bedroom.
